### Changes in this edition

This Vietnamese edition modifies the dataset: in `public/atlas/` the Chinese meanings, collocation glosses, tips and notes are replaced with Vietnamese translations (source files in `vi/translations/`), and the Chinese example translations are removed. The files in `data/` are the unmodified upstream dataset.
